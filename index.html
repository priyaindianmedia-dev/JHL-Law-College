Message from the Principal

Dear Learner,

Welcome to our esteemed law college. As you embark on this journey, rest assured that we are dedicated to your academic and personal growth. Our curriculum combines rigorous legal theory with practical experience, guided by our expert faculty, to give you a comprehensive understanding of law and its applications in the real world.

Beyond academics, we offer skill-building activities such as moot courts, workshops, and legal clinics to equip you for a dynamic career in law. We also provide personality and leadership programs to nurture your confidence, ethical values, and resilience.

I encourage you to engage fully in these opportunities, as they will shape you into not only a proficient lawyer but also a thoughtful leader. Wishing you a rewarding and transformative experience.

Warm regards, I/c Principal

Our Campus

Jaywant Harchand Lokhande Law College is located on Gorai Road, Borivali (West), Mumbai, and is affiliated to the University of Mumbai. Established in 2024, the college offers a supportive environment for legal education with moot courts, workshops and court visits.
