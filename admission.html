Admission

Detail About how to apply

We do not use special formulas to select students. We look at every single applicant’s application, academic and personal, to select students who suit our community with a full range of backgrounds.

The Application Process

1

Start Online Submission

Begin your application by contacting the admission office or using the enquiry form.

2

Submit The Form

Complete and submit the admission form with accurate details.

3

Review The Submission

The college reviews each application, academic and personal.

4

Gather Necessary Documents

Keep your academic records and identity documents ready for verification.

5

Interviewing Process

Shortlisted applicants may be called for a brief interaction.

6

Last Decision

You will be informed of the admission decision by the college.
