About JHL

Established in 2024, Jaywant Harchand Lokhande Law College has quickly emerged as a distinguished institution for legal education in Mumbai. The college is recognised as a popular choice for young, aspiring law students, offering a systematic and creative approach to learning. With a conducive and supportive environment, the institution aims to help students realize their full potential and develop the skills needed to excel in the field of law.

Run by Priyadarshinii Shaikshhanik & Samajik Vikas Mandal and affiliated to the University of Mumbai, the college is located on Gorai Road, Borivali (West), a well-connected part of Mumbai’s western suburbs. The college is named in memory of Late Shri Jaywant Harchand Lokhande, whose vision of accessible, quality education continues to guide the institution.

Our curriculum combines rigorous legal theory with practical experience, guided by dedicated faculty. Beyond the classroom, students take part in moot courts, workshops, legal clinics and regular court visits, which give first-hand exposure to judicial proceedings and courtroom procedure. Personality and leadership programmes, along with health, wellness and community service activities, help build confidence, ethical values and resilience.

The college is committed to fostering academic excellence and holistic growth, making it a preferred destination for legal studies.
